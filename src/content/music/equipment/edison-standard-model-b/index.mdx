I always wanted one of these. This one is a bit dirty but all the parts are free and it winds up and "works".

<Image
  src={topView}
  alt='a straight over the top view of an edison standard model b'
/>

The reproducer has a lot of probably decades old dust in some big clumps. I would imagine I will have to replace that. Even though the body is a Model B, the reproducer is Model C, which means it can only play a two minute cylinder I believe.

<Image
  src={topView2}
  alt='a slightly from the front, top view of an edison standard model b'
/>

The serial number dated it to 1907-1908.

<Image
  src={horn}
  alt='an upgraded larger horn for the edison standard'
/>

The end of the horn is a little damaged and will not connect to the reproducer at this time. If i get this cleaned up I would to prefer to get an original size horn.

I didn't know it when I picked it up, for these oversize horns, there was a little metal stand that would have a little chain connecting to the horn. It was probably there but I didn't know it was for this. Dammit!

Some of these are just cool as hell junk, some are actually in pretty good shape. Some old black wax, very dirty and moldy of course, mostly blue amberols. One or two Columbias in there too.
