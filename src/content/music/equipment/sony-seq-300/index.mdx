This was an estate sale find a year or two ago. I think I got it for like 15 bucks. It works great but the built in power outlet in the back, to power another device, it gets a little warm if I plug something in, but other than that it works great.

<br />
<br />

I feel like an EQ is like a pillow. Your pillow forms comfortably to your head. Without any change to it, it won't be as comfortable to my head. My EQ settings work for my middle aged damaged hearing, but probably won't sound as good to you and vice versa. To each their own...
