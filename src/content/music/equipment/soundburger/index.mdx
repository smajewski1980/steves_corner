This has been my daily machine for a year and a half at the time of this writing. I love this thing. I have a carrying case and have brought it on vacation and listened to records in the hotel room instead of having to wait to get them home. I even bought the nicer stylus too and switch them out depending on how nice the records are I am listening to.

<br />
<br />

I listen to anywhere from 1-3 records a day during the week and 5-10 on a weekend day. Doing the math, the stylus it came with should have worn out, it didn't really start sounding bad yet, but out of paranoia I bought another and replaced it some months back.

<br />
<br />

This thing is battery powered and has bluetooth too. The last time the power was out for a significant period, I was still listening to records on a little bluetooth speaker.
