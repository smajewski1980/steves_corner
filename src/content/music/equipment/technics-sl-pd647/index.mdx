This was a $14.99 Salvation Army find. It didn't even see the shelf, I saw a woman carrying it out, I said I'll take that please thank you very much...

It sits under a Sony single disc player on my desk at work and gets used regularly.

I had really wanted one of these because of the spiral feature. When spiral is on, it will play track one of all 5 discs, then track two of all 5, then track three... When I worked at a music store in my youth, we had one of these in the store. We would each pick a disc and it would do its thing. We would have everything from Enya to Puff Daddy going in that thing.
