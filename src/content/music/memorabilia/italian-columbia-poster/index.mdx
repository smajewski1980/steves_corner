This is a reproduction of an Italian advertisement for Columbia Phonographs which was originally from the 1920s. The artist was <a href='https://cappiello.fr/en/' target='_blank'>Leonetto Cappiello</a>, here is a link to a page maintained by his grandson. It sounds like he did over 2,000 pieces in his lifetime.

<br />

I got this at an estate sale and I believe it to be a reproduction from the 1960's. That's fine, it's huge and the colors still pop. It's a poster mounted on some really thick corrugated cardboard.
