So get this, this is like the only 80s one they put out that I didn't have on vinyl. I have it on CD, which includes their first E.P. Chronic Town with it on the disc.

I have been going to the same record store (big shoutout Last Vestige in Albany) for some years now as frequently as every week or two. Sometimes once a month. I always check the R.E.M. section when I go in usually to find the empty placeholder card. In The rare instances they have anything there I usually grab it. No matter what, I'm silly like that.

So the poster and one other item were shipped together but got seperated. The day the poster showed up, events led me to go to the record store that day too. As soon as I walked over to the R.E.M. section, there was a copy of Document out in front of that row that I could see as I approached. And I could also see this tiny sliver of a very distinct green color at top of a record just behind it. Holy shit, there it was! And the cooler of two versions which still has the hype sticker on it.
