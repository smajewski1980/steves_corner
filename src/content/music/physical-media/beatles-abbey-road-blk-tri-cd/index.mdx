I have gathered little pieces of the whole story from here and there online, heres the basics. 1978, Toshiba-EMI in Japan wanted to do a high end audiophile release of Abbey Road. So they were given a 30 ips flat copy of the orignal master tape by EMI UK.

<br />

1983 comes along and Toshiba-EMI, in order to help promote this new fangled CD business, they wanted a blockbuster title to release on the new format. They had the 1978 high end 30 ips tape in their vault and believed because EMI UK was their parent company, they owned the tape and could use it to make discs for their domestic market. So without permission thats just what they did.

<br />

EMI UK didn't like that. After a legal battle, the title was withdrawn and deleted.
