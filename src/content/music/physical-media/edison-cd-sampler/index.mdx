These are all scans of my actual disc and its liner notes. The back of the jewel case tray is just solid black.

This was released in small numbers at the opening of the CBS/Sony CD pressing plant in Terre Haute, Indiana, Digital Audio Disc Corporation in 1984.

<Image
  src={img2}
  alt=''
/>

Look at these hits! We get Teddy Roosevelt and William Jennings Bryan on the same disc. Even a little Sousa too.

<Image
  src={img4}
  alt=''
/>

Like this isn't rare enough, it originally came in a little pouch paired with Bruce Springsteen's "Born In The U.S.A." which had red label text. They say they ran off the Springsteen discs first, so they could say that the first disc they pressed in the U.S.A. was that.
