This is still sealed in the plastic. The last record ever pressed by the machines at the Universal pressing plant in Gloversville, NY before they went silent for good in 2005.

This was a plant that opened in the 30's and went from Brunswick to Decca to MCA to Universal. I only live around 30 miles away from where the plant was. I bought this on ebay from an address even closer.

I used to work with someone who had an open one of these. He said it is the signatures of all the employees at the plant pressed into the vinyl.
