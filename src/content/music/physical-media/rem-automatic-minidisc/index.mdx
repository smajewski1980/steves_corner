I remember when Minidiscs came out how cool the idea was to finally be able to make "mix-tapes" on a "disc". They were a little too pricey for me as a kid and then CD-r technology came out.

<br />
Around 1998 I was a live D.J. for a little while, the guy I worked for loved
these things. He could do a gig with a handful of these discs that he recorded
and send out his cds somewhere else.
<br />
Part of his advantage of having me onboard was I had my own cds.
