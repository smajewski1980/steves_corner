This CD came out in 2000, which is when I got into Less Than Jake. I saw them once. I found this promo CD Monday at the Salvation Army store and noticed it was definitely signed for real because you could see an imprint from the back of the paper for a few of them. Then when I got home and compared it online to something else that was signed by them around the same time, it matched up pretty well.

<Image
  src={salvArmy2}
  alt='A pile of cassettes of varying titles.'
/>

The Garth Brooks tape has a few tears in the plastic but is essentially still sealed... I thought the Rosette Cassette was interesting. The oldest blank tape specimen I have come across yet. On the back it has a place for an address and a stamp, of course it has sloppy scribbles and writing on it though.

<Iframe src='https://www.youtube.com/embed/FjxbxCG_9ZM?si=dCJ0uQMu-Bh7-UKR' />

Great score Monday at the Salvation Army. Except the Leslie Gore ones and the Rhapsody In Blue one, they came from the Re-Store.

I wanted the pile of empty 7" picture sleeves and was going to offer to pay half price since they were empty. I thought that was fair. I got as far as "These were sitting over there empty...", the girl was like "You can have them.", even better. I did look at the pile of loose 45s, I didn't see any that would have been reunited.
