I mean it does... but I more tell myself that to justify how much money I spend on music.

There's a group, <a href="https://www.8thstep.org/" target="_blank">The 8th Step</a><ExtLinkIcon />, who comes to the complex where I work twice a year to sell music for a fundraiser. It's a pretty large selection of records and CDs. It usually takes me an hour or so just to go through all the CDs. It's so nice to set my tablet with my catalog loaded up next to me while I rummage, and I can check if I have something before I buy it. After I made my first CD pass, I came in a little under budget, so I got a few records too. That's the selection in the cover shot above.
