I went to <a href='https://www.lastvestige.com/' target="_blank">Last Vestige</a><ExtLinkIcon /> to get some Graham Central Station, would you look at that, they had some! And for 5 bucks I had to take the Chuck Berry picture disc home too. I have a few Mountian albums, time to see what the Leslie West Band sounds like too.

<Image
  src={eponEmptyFront}
  alt="Two empty CD longboxes from R.E.M.'s Eponymous and Lifes Rich Pageant."

It was time to finsh my set of empty R.E.M. CD longboxes. All I needed was eponymous, but the only one listed by itself on eBay when I looked didn't have the hype sticker on it. So now I have another Life's Rich Pageant too.

<Image
  src={eponNew}
  alt="Front and rear view of a still sealed CD longbox of R.E.M.'s Eponymous"
/>

And yes we all fall for suggested advertising once in a while... When I bought the empty, it showed me a full. I now have two R.E.M. CDs sealed in the longbox. Again, there was one slightly cheaper but without the hype sticker. This user also had 100% feedback too, thats always a good selling point worth a few bucks more.

The Jerry Lee Lewis tape is OLD, I wish the label on it was in better shape. I was seven or so when La Bamba came out. My goodness, did I used to listen to that soundtrack a lot.
