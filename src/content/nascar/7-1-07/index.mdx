This was the first race I ever went to. I had been watching some races (the ones you didn't need cable for) on and off for a number of years, but was just starting to become a serious fan and following things a little closer over the year or two before this race.

A friend of mine at work who took his daughter to Loudon for years, had an extra ticket after she grew up and got married and pregnant and couldn't go. He offered it to me.

This was nineteen years ago as I write this, and one memory I still have and will always have is when we were walking to our seats and Dave said lets go out and walk along the fence down the frontstretch.

That very first time cracking through the dark shadows from under the stands to come out to the track, there was an almost angelic ray of light casting down on those colorful and ready racecars staged on pitroad. (lets remember, HD tv was just starting to catch on at this point...)

It is a memory I will forever keep next to the first time walking out in the old Yankee Stadium when I was a kid and seeing the familiar image of the field that you saw on tv..
