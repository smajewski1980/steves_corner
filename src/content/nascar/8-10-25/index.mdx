Shane Van Gisbergen is a machine on these road courses! I went to this one to see him win, and wasn't disappointed.

We sat at the last turn, turn 7, which sits at the end of a very short straight which has the cars coming straight at the grandstand for a moment. It's a shame we didn't take any pictures.

Beyond turn 6, there are trees and other sight limiting factors. It was so cool to hear the cars getting closer, getting louder, and then every lap you would hear a car breaking away and every time they would come around, no no, let me rephrase that. Every time SVG would come around turn 6, the wait was longer and longer to see the rest of the field.
