Tonight is this years fall Richmond race, I figured might as well do this one today.

This is one of those more recent races that I guess we didn't take any pics at.

But I can offer this...

This was one of the craziest endings to a race. Austin Dillon dominated the evening. Then he screws up on the last restart. What does he do? For the win, he decides to go bowling and pick up the spare taking out the top 2 instead of facing his mistake and finishing 3rd.

I was ok with Logano getting the win. Last lap, goin into turn 3, Austin Dillon drove so hard into turn 3, he never had any intention of making the corner without taking Logano out.

From our seats I could see it all perfect. We were near the flagstand, 20 rows up. What a roller coaster of emotion for ten seconds.

I was pissed seeing driving like that, but then after Logano got into the wall and as Dillon is getting his traction back from sliding, wait, what's this, here comes Denny Hamlin!

From our angle, I was staring at Hamlin's nose pointed straight at me coming to take the checkers, then in an instant he was perpendicular to the wall and here comes Dillon to the checkered flag.

They play the ambient noise from the crowd when they show the finish on tv. When I watched it back, I can totally hear myself and one other person that was across the aisle from me, booing as loud as possible.

I may have let some explitives fly towards Austin Dillon and I gave him a nice one finger salute when he did his victory lap.

Do you think he could get a ride from a non family owned team?
