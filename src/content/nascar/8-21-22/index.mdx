This was a miserable day. This is the only picture we wound up with I guess.

To start with, the track never told anyone that the week before the race, none of the concessions were going to accept cash anymore. So here I was thinking we'd be fine for the day with cash in my pocket(you know, like we had been for over a decade at this point of going to many races), no need to bring any cards in with us.

Wrong, let's walk the mile plus back to the car to come a mile plus back in again.

Then mother nature chimed in.... It was raining, but either way rain or not, when there is lightning within a certain distance of the track, they will clear the stands out until it passes.

So where do all those people go when its raining?

Lightning shmightning! Instead of sitting ON the metal stands, everybody goes UNDER the metal stands to stay dry.

Whatever...

Nothing beats being packed in under the stands with everyone else thats aggrivated its raining out trying to pass the time...

This was the first race with the NextGen car with the rain tires on. The rain eventually let up enough and they raced, on dry tires after a little while of racing on the wets.
